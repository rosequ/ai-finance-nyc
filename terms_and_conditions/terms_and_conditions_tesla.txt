General Terms and Conditions Agreement between Tesla Motors, Inc. and Panasonic Corporation (October 1, 2014)
Contract Categories: Business Operations › Supply Agreements
Summary

This agreement sets out the terms under which Tesla Motors, Inc. engages Panasonic Corporation to develop, supply, and support products for use in Tesla's products. Panasonic will also lease space within Tesla's manufacturing facility. The contract covers production planning, order processes, and the obligations of both parties regarding forecasts, purchase orders, and production meetings. It specifies how orders are placed, accepted, and adjusted, and outlines the process for investment in manufacturing property. The agreement is effective as of October 1, 2014, and governs the ongoing business relationship between the two companies.

These General Terms and Conditions (“General Terms”) are entered into effective as of October 1, 2014 (“Effective Date”) by and between Tesla Motors, Inc., a Delaware corporation located at 3500 Deer Creek Road, Palo Alto, California, 94304 (“Tesla”), on the one hand, and Panasonic Corporation (“Seller”), on the other hand.
